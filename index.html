Decision lens

Separates Fit (“is this the right company?”) from Readiness (“is this the right time?”). This does not replace the existing 0–100 Focus Score.

—Contact now · high fit / high readiness
—Monitor · high fit / lower readiness
—Review fit · lower fit / high readiness

How the new lens is calculated

Fit (provisional): training evidence 25, safety evidence 25, expansion evidence 20, subvertical 15, enrichment 15, less 30 for profile-review flag. Readiness: existing tier + signal points, normalized to 100. Threshold for “high” is 70. The original Focus Score and ranking are not changed.
